### 🏋️ Case Study: Fitness Activity, Sleep, and Heart Rate
**Tools:** R, ggplot2, dplyr, tidyr  
**Data:** Fitbit wearable device data  

**Highlights:**
- Aggregated and merged datasets on sleep, steps, heart rate.
- Time-series trend analysis on daily activity.
- Regression modeling to predict sleep duration.
- Visualizations:
  - Distribution of sleep minutes
  - Steps vs. Calories with regression line
  - Steps vs. Heart Rate
  - Sedentary vs. Heart Rate

**Value:** Helped uncover behavioral patterns for Bellabeat to support wellness product development.
